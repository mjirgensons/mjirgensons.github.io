## Ways to reach Martin Jirgenson

- **GitHub**: open an issue or discussion in the [jirgenson-model repository](https://github.com/mjirgensons/jirgenson-model) for anything about the canon, the prompt or the blind test.
- **Gumroad**: message through the [author's Gumroad page](https://jirgenstorm4.gumroad.com).
- **Email**: coming shortly; until then the two channels above reach the author directly.

## What to send

- **A review.** Published on the [reviews page](/reviews/) with your name or initials, as you prefer, including critical reviews.
- **A correction.** If a number, a definition or a claim in the book or on this site is wrong, say which and why. Corrections are listed in the book's version history.
- **Press and podcasts.** The [press kit](/press/) has the facts, the numbers with their limits and the bio. Review copies (PDF) on request.
