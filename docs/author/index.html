What I have built

My background spans from custom manufacturing to full stack software engineering.

  • Education software at national scale. I developed a school management platform that serves over 90 percent of schools across Latvia.
  • Manufacturing operations. I built custom ERP software to manage 700,000 square feet of production space.
  • Sales. I have directed B2B sales strategies.
  • AI for operations. Today I focus on the North American market as the founder of VocaIQ, delivering managed AI voice agents for B2B operations.

The thread through all of it is the same: a system is only as good as the numbers it reads and the speed at which it corrects course.

Why I wrote this book

I wrote Steer, Don't Guess specifically for business owners, CEOs and COOs. People who are used to taking responsibility: for their business, their team, their family, and the promises they have made.

You probably know the feeling. You do a great deal, you solve problems, you try hard. Yet you cannot always explain why some efforts pay off while others vanish without a trace. Everything holds together only as long as you personally attend to every detail.

The book is not abstract theory. It is a direct, practical manual built on decades of hands-on experience. It shows you exactly how to read your numbers, adjust your strategy and steer your operations through complex business realities.

What I believe about management

  • Test it, do not believe it. I do not ask you to take the method on faith. Every chapter invites you to check it against your own numbers, and the book states its own limits.
  • To improve does not always mean to increase. A business can grow and become more fragile at the same time. Decide first what you are improving, for whom and at what price.
  • People must not disappear behind the diagram. An owner's time is not a free resource. A team's fatigue is not only a productivity factor.
  • Plain language, honest about complexity. The first step needs no special training. Some problems still need a calculation, an experiment or professional advice.

What is the Jirgenson Model?

A management method from control theory: an ordered list of measurable goals, the same list filled with measured facts, and levers to close the gap, with zones, red lines and forecasts. It is set out in Steer, Don't Guess and in a free canon for AI assistants.
