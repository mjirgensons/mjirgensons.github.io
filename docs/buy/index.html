Questions people ask

Is the Kindle edition the same as the paperback?

Yes, the same text, edition 1.1. Kindle reports 264 pages because of its typesetting; the print editions have 237.

Can I buy it outside the US and Canada?

Yes. The same editions are listed on every Amazon marketplace; use the ISBN or the links above. The PDF on Gumroad is available worldwide.

Is there a refund?

For Amazon purchases, Amazon's return policy applies. For the PDF, read the free 44-page sample first: it contains the whole of Chapter 1, so you know what you are buying.
