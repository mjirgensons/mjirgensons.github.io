Write to me

I read every message myself, and I reply personally, as the author. Questions about the method, a number that looks wrong, a situation from your own business or family: all of it is welcome.

What to send

  • A review. Published on the reviews page with your name or initials, as you prefer, including critical reviews.
  • A correction. If a number, a definition or a claim in the book or on this site is wrong, say which and why. Corrections are listed in the book’s version history.
  • Press and podcasts. The press kit has the facts, the numbers with their limits and the bio. Review copies (PDF) on request.
