Contact the author

Reviews, press and podcast requests, questions about the model, corrections. Critical feedback is welcome; the book's own rule is that the author promises nothing and corrects what is wrong.

Ways to reach Martin Jirgenson

  • GitHub: open an issue or discussion in the jirgenson-model repository for anything about the canon, the prompt or the blind test.
  • Gumroad: message through the author’s Gumroad page.
  • Email: coming shortly; until then the two channels above reach the author directly.

What to send

  • A review. Published on the reviews page with your name or initials, as you prefer, including critical reviews.
  • A correction. If a number, a definition or a claim in the book or on this site is wrong, say which and why. Corrections are listed in the book’s version history.
  • Press and podcasts. The press kit has the facts, the numbers with their limits and the bio. Review copies (PDF) on request.
